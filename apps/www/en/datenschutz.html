2. The short version

  • All of nuved's services run on servers we rent from Hetzner in Germany. Sign-in, email, git and debugging run there under our own operation, with no further provider.
  • Every nuved web address goes through Cloudflare, which passes requests on to our servers and protects them from attacks.
  • The nuved.io website sets no cookies, loads no scripts and nothing from anyone else's servers. There is no tracking, no advertising, no analytics, and we sell no data.
  • Payments happen on Stripe's payment page. We never see your card details.
  • Backups are kept at Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt and, encrypted with keys only we hold, in a private repository on GitHub.

3. Where the services run

Hetzner

Our servers are cloud servers of Hetzner Online GmbH, [[HETZNER_ANSCHRIFT]], in the Nuremberg data centre (console, sign-in, relay, git, website, debugging) and the Falkenstein data centre (mail server). Hetzner runs the hardware and the network; we run the software on it. Hetzner processes everything stored on our servers on our behalf (Article 28 GDPR).

Cloudflare

Every nuved web address (nuved.io, console.nuved.io, api.nuved.io, auth.nuved.io, git.nuved.io, nuved.app, relay.fromnovid.com and the share addresses under fromnovid.com) points at Cloudflare. Cloudflare accepts your connection, decrypts it, filters attacks and passes the request on to our server. In doing so Cloudflare processes your IP address, the address requested, the request headers (such as your browser) and the request's content. The DNS of our domains is at Cloudflare too. The first page of nuved.io is served by a small program at Cloudflare, so that it still appears when our server is briefly unavailable; it sets no cookies and keeps no log of its own.

The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., [[CLOUDFLARE_ANSCHRIFT]], USA. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest is that the services are reachable and protected from attacks. On the transfer to the USA, see section 14. One exception: ssh.nuved.io (git over SSH) connects you to our server directly, without Cloudflare.

4. When you visit nuved.io

The nuved.io pages are static. They set no cookies, contain no JavaScript, and their fonts and images come from our own server. When you open them, your browser talks to nobody but Cloudflare and us.

Server logs

The web server that receives requests for our services writes one line per request: the time, your IP address (as Cloudflare passes it on), Cloudflare's request id, the method and address of the page requested, the response code, the bytes sent, the page you came from (referrer), your browser's identification (user agent) and the response time. We use these lines to find faults and to detect and fend off attacks (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). They are deleted after [[LOG_FRIST]]; we build no profiles from them and combine them with no other data.

Review note: There is no fixed period today. The lines go to Docker's container log, which rotates by size (3 files of 10 MB, measured on 7 October 2026); that day the oldest entry was from 16 September 2026, about three weeks. The same web server also serves the operator's other projects. Recommendation: set a fixed maximum (say 14 days) technically and name it here, or describe the criterion (Article 13(2)(a) GDPR).

5. Account and sign-in

Sign-in and accounts run through our own sign-in service at auth.nuved.io (the open-source software authentik, on our server). At the moment an account can only be created by invitation.

  • When you create it: username, name, email address and password. We don't store the password in clear text, only a hash of it.
  • Optionally: a second sign-in factor (authenticator app, passkey or recovery codes), if you set one up.
  • At every sign-in: an event log with the time, your IP address, your browser's identification and the approximate location and network operator derived from them. That derivation happens on our server with a local database; nothing is sent to anyone.

The purpose is to give you an account and sign you in securely (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and to detect misuse of the account (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Confirmation, invitation and password reset emails are sent through our own mail server (section 8). auth.nuved.io does not offer signing in with a GitHub account.

Account and profile data stay until the account is deleted. Sign-in events are deleted after [[AUTHENTIK_EREIGNIS_FRIST]].

Review note: How long sign-in events are kept is a setting in authentik that was not read here; the default is a year as far as I recall (not re-checked). The console has no self-service way to delete one's own account today (checked in the code on 7 October 2026); deletion is on request.

6. Console, API and command line

console.nuved.io, api.nuved.io and the nuved command line are one application on our server, with its own database. It stores what the service needs:

  • your organization, its owner's email address, and its members with their role and the projects they may see;
  • invitations: the invited email address and who sent the invitation; if the invited person accepts and creates an organization of their own, we record the referral;
  • access tokens, only as a SHA-256 checksum, with their name and the email address of the person who created them; SSH keys for checking commit signatures, with the email address a signature carries;
  • projects, services and their addresses;
  • an activity log for your organization: who (a person, an agent or the system) did what, when, and with what result;
  • the balance, every entry (top-up, usage, credit) and the usage per project;
  • settings for balance alerts, and when the last alert was sent.

The command line signs in through auth.nuved.io: you confirm a code in your browser. It sends no usage statistics.

The legal basis is our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We send an invitation to another person because a member of your organization asks us to; the legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) is that teams can work together. We keep accounting entries as long as tax and commercial law require (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR with § 147(1) no. 4 and (3) AO: eight years for accounting vouchers, [[HGB_FRIST_FALLS_KAUFMANN]]). Everything else stays as long as your organization exists and is deleted afterwards, unless a retention duty applies.

7. Paying

You top up your balance on Stripe's payment page (Stripe Checkout). You enter your card details there, not with us. We give Stripe the amount, your organization and your email address. Stripe gives us back a customer id, whether and when the payment arrived, the link to Stripe's receipt, and a fingerprint of the card. The fingerprint is an identifier the card number can't be derived from; we use it only to stop someone from earning a referral credit from themselves with the same card.

The provider is [[STRIPE_VERTRAGSPARTNER_UND_ANSCHRIFT]]. Stripe processes payment data partly on our behalf and partly as a controller in its own right, for example to prevent fraud and to meet its own legal duties; Stripe's privacy policy applies to that (stripe.com/privacy). The legal basis is our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR); for comparing fingerprints, our legitimate interest in preventing misuse of credits (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Review note: During the preview Stripe runs in test mode; no real money moves. The contracting party for EEA accounts is, as I recall, Stripe Payments Europe, Limited (Ireland), not re-checked; please confirm the role split (processor / own controller) against Stripe's current contract documents.

8. Email

We send and receive email through our own mail server (Mailu, on a Hetzner server in Falkenstein). It sends invitations, confirmations, password reset links, balance alerts and the sign-in codes for guests of shares (section 9). Mail is sent from auth@nuved.io; guests' sign-in codes currently come from auth@fromnovid.com. The logo in our emails is loaded from nuved.io at an address that is the same for every recipient, so we don't measure who opened an email. If your mail program loads images, Cloudflare and our server log see your IP address when it does (section 4). The emails with guest codes load nothing.

If you write to us, we process your address and your message in order to reply (Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete them when the matter is settled, at the latest after [[MAIL_FRIST]], unless a retention duty applies (for business letters, § 147(1) nos. 2 and 3 and (3) AO: six years).

9. Shares and guests

With a share, someone makes a service on their own computer reachable at an address <organization>-<name>.fromnovid.com. The traffic goes through our relay.

When you share something

The relay stores your organization, your computer's public key and internal address, when it last connected, which shares it offers and, per day and share, how many requests and bytes it had. The purpose is to run the share and to apply your plan's traffic limits (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

When you are invited as a guest

The person who runs a share decides who may open it, and enters email addresses for that. When you open the share, you enter your address and receive a code by email. The relay stores:

WhatHolds your address?Until when
the entry on the invite list (when added, when last used)yesuntil the person removes it, the share's address lapses, it goes unused for 90 days, or you opt out or report the invitation
the sign-in code, only as a SHA-256 checksumyesa day at most

"no more codes to me" and "I don't know this person"no, only as a keyed checksum (HMAC-SHA256)until you ask for it to be lifted; a report counts towards a pause for 30 days

After sign-in, the relay sets a cookie that keeps you signed in to that one share for 30 days (section 15). Every email with a code carries a link to stop further codes, or to report that you don't know the person who invited you.

The relay passes your request, including your IP address, on to the shared service. What that service does with it is the responsibility of the person who runs it, not of nuved.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest, and that of the person sharing, in limiting access to the share to invited people, and yours in not being written to unasked (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

10. Code and GitHub

Projects that run on nuved live in our own git service (git.nuved.io, ssh.nuved.io). With the code we store what git records for every commit, including the author's name and email address.

If you connect a project to a GitHub repository, we create a key for it that you add on GitHub, and from then on copy every state of main to that repository. That happens on your instruction (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR); at GitHub the code then sits in your account, under your contract with GitHub.

12. Debugging

The console and the relay write technical traces to a tool on our own server (Jaeger). A trace holds: a request's method, route and response code, its duration, the name of the organization and the service, the kind of email sent, and the outcome. It never holds email addresses, IP addresses, browser identification, full addresses or content: anything not on a fixed list of allowed fields is dropped before it leaves the service. Of the traffic through shares, only about one request in a hundred is traced. Traces are deleted after seven days. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in finding faults and keeping the services stable (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

13. Backups

Once a day we back up the databases of the console, the sign-in service, the relay and the git service, so that nothing is lost after a failure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, Article 32 GDPR).

  • Amazon Web Services (S3), Frankfurt region. The last three daily backups per service; a replaced backup is deleted a day later. Relay and git service backups are encrypted with our own keys before upload; the console's and the sign-in service's [[S3_VERSCHLUESSELUNG_KONSOLE_AUTHENTIK]]. The provider is [[AWS_VERTRAGSPARTNER_UND_ANSCHRIFT]].
  • GitHub, private repository. A third copy of the last three days, encrypted with keys only we hold; GitHub can't read it. The provider is GitHub, Inc., [[GITHUB_ANSCHRIFT]], USA.
  • On our server. The relay also keeps its last seven backups.
Review note: As of 7 October 2026 (read in platform-cluster backup-github/README.md): the console's and the sign-in service's backups sit in S3 with AWS's server-side encryption only (SSE-S3), not with our own keys; AWS could technically read them. Either change the setup (age before upload, as for the relay) or keep the text as it stands and assess the transfer accordingly. Under aws-teardown/ there is also a final copy of the former AWS environment (the console, git service and LiteLLM databases and the customers' git repositories, as of 6 October 2026) with no deletion period.

17. Your rights

You have the right of access (Article 15 GDPR), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction of processing (Article 18), data portability (Article 20) and to object (Article 21). You can withdraw any consent you gave at any time, for the future (Article 7(3)). Write to [[DATENSCHUTZ_EMAIL]].

Right to object

Where we process data on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article 21(1) GDPR). We then stop, unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), in particular in the Member State where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place, or with the authority responsible for us: [[AUFSICHTSBEHOERDE_NAME_ANSCHRIFT]].

We make no decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Article 22 GDPR).
