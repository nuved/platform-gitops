Review note: § 36(1) no. 1 VSBG: a business with a website tells consumers whether it takes part in proceedings before a consumer arbitration body. A business with ten or fewer employees on 31 December of the previous year is exempt (§ 36(3) VSBG), and only from no. 1. The usual wording when it doesn't take part: "We are neither willing nor obliged to take part in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration body."
