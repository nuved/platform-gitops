Questions

Short answers. The longer version is on how it works.

What do I get?

A set number of isolated environments, however many your plan allows. You create them yourself, deploy into them, and delete them when you are done.

How do I deploy?

Push to a git repository. The environment updates itself to match what is in the repository, so reverting a commit rolls the deploy back.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the billing portal, without asking anyone. Cancelling removes your environments and everything in them, so take what you need first.

Is my data isolated?

Each environment has its own limits and its own access rules, and cannot reach another. Nothing leaves an environment unless you publish it yourself.
